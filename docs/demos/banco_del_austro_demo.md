# Banco del Austro – Full Strategy Campaign Demo

**Duration:** ~8 minutes  
**Client:** Banco del Austro (Ecuador)  
**Objective:** Digital Transformation & Gen-Z Banking Campaign

---

## Script

### 1. Launch (30s)
- Open Inhaus Brain dashboard
- Click the **"Banco del Austro – Full Strategy Campaign"** glassmorphic button
- Point out: "This activates demo mode and pre-loads client data"

### 2. Strategy Phase (2 min)
- Observe the AI router classify the brief as a **strategy** task
- Watch the Blackboard transition from `idle → strategy`
- Point out the Strategy Agent generating market analysis
- Show the Flight Recorder panel (real-time agent status)

### 3. Research & Grounding (1.5 min)
- Watch grounding search results populate the Knowledge base
- Show the Research Workbench with extracted entities
- Highlight budget, timeline, and audience parameters

### 4. Creative Generation (2 min)
- Watch the Copywriter Agent generate campaign copy (ES/EN)
- Trigger image generation with Imagen 3
- Show GenUI rendering: strategy_board + timeline components
- Optional: trigger Veo video preview (15s clip)

### 5. Proposal PDF (1.5 min)
- Navigate to Agency → Proposals
- Show AI-generated proposal with pricing, timeline, deliverables
- Export PDF with one click
- Highlight margin calculation

### 6. Approval Flow (30s)
- Click "Approve" on the proposal
- Watch Blackboard transition to `approved`
- Show analytics event `human_approval` firing in debug console
