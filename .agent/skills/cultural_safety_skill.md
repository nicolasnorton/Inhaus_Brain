# Cultural Safety & Sensitivity Skill

## Purpose
To guarantee that all produced content (text, image, video) respects the cultural nuances of Ecuador and Latin America, avoiding stereotypes, bias, or culturally irrelevant metaphors.

## Application Rules
**Apply this skill when:**
- Generating marketing copy, campaigns, or social media posts for LatAm audiences.
- Creating visual assets (images/video) depicting people or environments in LatAm.
- Translating concepts from English to Spanish.

## Core Guidelines

### 1. Geographic Specificity (Ecuador First)
- **Primary Context**: Ecuador.
- **Secondary**: Andean Region & General LatAm.
- **Avoid**: Generic "Mexicanized" Spanish (e.g., avoid "güey", "chido", "cuate" unless specifically targeting Mexico).
- **Use**: Neutral, professional Latin American Spanish ("usted" for formal, "tú" for calculated affinity, never "vos" unless specifically requested for casual distinctiveness).

### 2. Visual Representation
- **Diversity**: Reflect the true demographic diversity of Ecuador (Mestizo, Afro-Ecuadorian, Indigenous, Euro-descendant).
- **Environments**: Use accurate settings (Andes mountains, coastal beaches, Amazon rainforest, modern urban Quito/Guayaquil) rather than generic "tropical" stereotypes.
- **Avoid Stereotypes**: No sombreros, maracas, or sepia filters unless culturally justified.

### 3. Tone & Nuance
- **Respect**: High respect for local traditions and holidays (e.g., Fiestas de Quito, Carnaval, Día de los Difuntos).
- **Modesty**: Avoid overly aggressive sales tactics; prefer relationship-building and value-forward communication.
- **Inclusivity**: Ensure gender-neutral language where possible ("Hola a todos" or "Bienvenidos").

## Verification Steps
1. **Language Check**: Is the Spanish specifically Ecuadorian/Neutral? (No slang from other regions).
2. **Imagery Check**: Does the visual representation align with the target demographic?
3. **Tone Check**: Is the tone respectful and culturally appropriate?
