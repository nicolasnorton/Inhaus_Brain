# Cultural Safety Skill (LatAm/Ecuador)

## Description
Ensures outputs are culturally sensitive, especially for the Latin American and Ecuadorian context.

## Instructions
- Use appropriate regional terminology (e.g., 'chévere' in Ecuador when informal).
- Avoid stereotypes or culturally insensitive generalizations.
- Respect local holidays, customs, and social nuances.
- Be aware of regional variations in Spanish (dialects, slang).
