# Audience Targeting Taxonomy

## Funnel Tier Definitions

| Tier | Temperature | Behavior Signal | Recency Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cold | Prospecting | No prior interaction | N/A |
| Warm | Consideration | Engaged with brand | 30–90 days |
| Hot | Conversion | High-intent action | 7–30 days |

## Platform Audience Minimums

| Platform | Cold Min | Retargeting Min | Lookalike Source Min |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Meta | 1,000 | 100 | 100 (for 1%) |
| TikTok | 1,000 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| LinkedIn | 300 | 300 | 300 |

## Interest Category Clusters (LatAm / General)

### eCommerce / Retail
- Online shopping, fashion, beauty, home decor, electronics.

### B2B / Professional Services
- Business decision makers, company size >50, industry verticals, LinkedIn job titles.

### Health & Wellness
- Fitness, nutrition, wellness, health & beauty.

### Real Estate
- Home buyers, renters, real estate investors, property management.

### Finance
- Personal finance, investing, small business owners, entrepreneurs.

## Retargeting Audience Hierarchy

1. **Purchasers (90-day)** → Upsell / cross-sell campaigns.
2. **Checkout abandoners (7-day)** → Urgency offer; highest priority.
3. **Add-to-cart (14-day)** → Reminder + social proof.
4. **Product page viewers (30-day)** → Consideration content.
5. **Video viewers 75%+ (60-day)** → Interest nurturing.
6. **All website visitors (30-day)** → Brand reminder.

## Exclusion Rules

| Campaign Type | Must Exclude |
| :--- | :--- |
| Prospecting | All purchasers, all warm/hot segments |
| MOFU retargeting | Purchasers, checkout abandoners |
| BOFU retargeting | Purchasers (unless repurchase campaign) |
| Lookalike | Seed audience itself |

## Lookalike Best Practices

- **Best seed**: Top 10% LTV customers (value-based lookalike).
- **Minimum seed size**: 100 unique users (Meta), 300 (LinkedIn), 1,000 (TikTok).
- **Start with 1%** lookalike; scale to 2–3% only after ROAS is proven.
- **Refresh seed** every 30–60 days to capture new buyers.
