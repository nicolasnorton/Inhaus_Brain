# Campaign Budget Frameworks

## 70/20/10 Allocation Rule

| Bucket | % | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Core | 70% | Proven channels with demonstrated ROAS |
| Growth | 20% | Scaling experiments with positive signals |
| Innovation | 10% | New formats, untested audiences, creative bets |

## Channel Minimum Viable Budgets (USD/month)

| Platform | Min for Learning | Recommended Minimum |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Meta Ads | $300 | $1,000 |
| TikTok Ads | $500 | $1,500 |
| LinkedIn Ads | $1,000 | $3,000 |
| Google Ads | $500 | $2,000 |

## Funnel Stage KPI Targets

| Stage | Primary KPI | Secondary KPI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| TOFU | CPM < $8 | Video View Rate > 25% |
| MOFU | CTR > 1.5% | Engagement Rate > 3% |
| BOFU | ROAS > 2× | CVR > 2% |

## LatAm Market Notes

- Ecuador peak ad hours: 7–9 AM and 8–10 PM local time.
- WhatsApp integration (Meta Click-to-WhatsApp) often outperforms standard lead forms in LatAm.
- Spanish creative consistently outperforms English-language ads by 15–30% in LatAm markets.
